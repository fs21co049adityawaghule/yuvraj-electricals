Our Story

Who We Are

Yuvraj Electricals is an industrial electrical services business providing panel wiring, panel assembly, busbar assembly, panel fabrication, and PLC/VFD automation panel work. The business is operated by Yuvraj Shelar, who focuses on delivering practical electrical panel work for industrial customers.

We work directly with clients to understand their requirements, confirm scope clearly before starting, and carry out the work with attention to quality and organization — clean wiring, proper ferrule labelling, and thorough checks.

Our work covers a wide range of industrial panel types — from motor control centres and power control centres to PLC panels, VFD panels, APFC panels, and distribution boards.

Our Work

What We Do

Yuvraj Electricals covers the full range of industrial electrical panel services — from raw enclosure work to complete panel wiring and automation integration.

🔌

Panel Wiring

Power wiring, control wiring, PLC/VFD wiring, relay and contactor wiring, terminal and ferrule wiring — for all types of industrial control panels.

🔧

Panel Assembly

Component mounting, layout, and complete panel assembly for MCC, PCC, PLC, VFD, APFC, and distribution panels.

⚡

Busbar Assembly

Copper and aluminium busbar cutting, drilling, mounting, insulation, and power distribution busbar assembly.

🖥️

Automation Panel Work

PLC panel wiring, VFD panel wiring, HMI connections, sensor/actuator wiring, and PLC-VFD integration wiring.

🚪

Door Cutout & Glass

Panel door cutouts for push buttons, indicators, meters, HMI displays, and glass fitting with proper alignment.

🏗️

Panel Fabrication

Custom enclosure fabrication, door fabrication, mounting plate preparation, and mechanical panel modifications.
