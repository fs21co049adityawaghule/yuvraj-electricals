What We Do

From panel wiring to full PLC/VFD integration — we cover the complete range of industrial electrical panel services.

Panel Wiring

Industrial control, power, motor control, MCC, PCC, PLC, VFD, and relay wiring with clean organization and proper ferrule labelling.

Panel Assembly

Complete panel assembly from component mounting to full electrical assembly — MCC, PCC, PLC, VFD, APFC, and distribution panels.

Busbar Assembly
